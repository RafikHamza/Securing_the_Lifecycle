# Open Issues Before Submission

1. The current development and diagnostic corpora are engineering probes, not a blind external benchmark. A new frozen corpus is required before making general precision, recall, or superiority claims.
2. Semgrep has not been executed in the retained evidence set. Do not report a Semgrep comparison until the executable, rules, raw JSON output, and exact corpus are archived.
3. The paper evaluates the Python AST analyzer. Native libclang and C/C++ analysis remain optional artifact capabilities and are outside the present evidence scope.
4. The real-cryptography timing covers two complete scenario outcomes per repetition. It must not be presented as an isolated CKKS or ML-KEM primitive latency.
5. The Raspberry Pi 4 measurements cover static source verification only. They are not end-to-end device-edge-cloud latency.
6. No aggregate ESP32-S3 performance claim is currently approved.
7. Author order, affiliations, acknowledgments, biographies, and the final public artifact URL remain to be inserted.
